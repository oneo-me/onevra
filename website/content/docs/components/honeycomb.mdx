---
title: Honeycomb
description: Items on a magnified honeycomb, as on a watch face, panned by dragging.
---

<Example name="honeycomb" height={420} />

Drag or scroll to pan; the honeycomb settles with the item nearest the middle centered and chosen. Items are magnified most in the middle and turn away toward the edges, as if on the surface of a ball. The keyboard reaches every item.

Items draw their own content at the given size, or `Images` overlapping as a group with a badge counting those beyond three. `Caption` names the middle item on a glass label, and `Curvature`, `Gap` and `OffsetY` tune the layout.
